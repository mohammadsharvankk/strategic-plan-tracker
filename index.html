    Method

    Achievement = projected Q4 ÷ target (for KPIs where higher is better), or target ÷ projected Q4 (where lower is better), capped at 120%.

    Projection = Q3 value + the average quarterly change from Q1 to Q3, scaled by scenario: pessimistic × 0.4, base × 1.0, optimistic × 1.5.

    Status: on track ≥ 98%, at risk 90–98%, off track < 90%. Initiatives are delayed when 5–15 points behind plan and critical when more than 15 points behind.
